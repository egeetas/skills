# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We want this project to be a welcoming place for focused technical collaboration.
Be respectful, assume good intent, critique ideas with evidence, and make room for
people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory
language, deliberate intimidation, publishing another person's private
information, spam, and sustained disruption of project discussions.

## Scope and enforcement

This standard applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, and other
spaces where someone represents this project. Maintainers may edit or remove
content and temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior harms
the community.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through the contact
method shown on their GitHub profile. Include relevant links or screenshots and
avoid sharing the report publicly. Reports will be handled with discretion, though
confidentiality cannot be guaranteed in every legal or safety circumstance.
