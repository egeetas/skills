# Grader rubric checklist

For every judgment-heavy dimension, define:

- the observable property being scored;
- an ordinal scale with behaviorally distinct anchors;
- examples near each decision boundary;
- critical failures that override an aggregate score;
- how abstentions, invalid outputs, and missing evidence are handled;
- whether the grader sees candidate identity or order;
- calibration cases and inter-rater agreement checks;
- a policy for adjudication and rubric changes.

Do not let style preferences stand in for task success. Keep the rubric frozen within a comparison run and version it with the dataset and prompt.
