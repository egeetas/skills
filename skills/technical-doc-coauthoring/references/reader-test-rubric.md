# Reader-test rubric

Give a fresh reviewer only the document and ask them to answer:

1. What decision or action does this document request?
2. Who owns the next step, and what are its prerequisites?
3. Which facts, assumptions, and open questions can you distinguish?
4. What are the main alternatives and why was one selected?
5. What can fail, how will it be detected, and how is it rolled back?
6. Which terms, commands, links, or examples are ambiguous or unverifiable?
7. What important question would a new implementer or operator still need answered?

Treat the review as evidence, not authority. Verify comments, deduplicate them, and revise only when they improve correctness or reader actionability.
